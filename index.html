Greek Reader

Tap a word in your own text and type its meaning to save it. The built-in texts have meanings ready.

Listen-and-repeat drill, in the style of Glossika: you hear the English sentence, then the Greek several times, with a pause each time for you to say it aloud.

Saved words

In Anki choose File, then Import, and pick the exported .txt file (Anki 2.1.54 or newer reads its settings automatically). The front of each card is the Greek word. The back has the meaning, the grammar of the form you tapped, and your example sentence.
